# Lantai 兰台

Much of our work now goes to agents, and by evening most of the day lives in conversations. What we did outside them, whether things worked out, what we made of it: only we know.

Lantai puts both into one journal. It reads the sessions that agents such as Claude Code and Codex CLI leave on your machine; a model drafts daily reports and rolls them up into weekly and monthly ones, every point linked to its conversation. Beside them, you write what no session holds. The two hands stay distinct, and regenerating never touches what you wrote. Your data stays on your Mac: Lantai goes online only to send redacted material to the model you chose, and to check for updates when you ask.

A record like this may be of little use to the agents, but it matters to us: looking back over the days, you see how much you got done working alongside them.

Lantai (兰台) was where the Han court kept its books and archives; lan (兰) is the orchid. Gathering each day's scattered work and filing it away is what this app sets out to do.
